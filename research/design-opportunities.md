# Design opportunities

1. **Type-led identity instead of JPG banners.** Live Hebrew display typography carries campaigns; images carry product and people. Accessible, responsive, editable per season.
2. **One brand voice in Hebrew.** Replace stray English headings ("Join Us", "Secure Payments", "MEN") with Hebrew; keep Latin only for brand names and the Gali wordmark.
3. **A single, confident hero.** The family campaign photo leads; the season-end sale moves to its own designed moment further down rather than competing at the top.
4. **Tell the three truths the current site hides:** since 1975 (heritage), the whole family under one roof (women/men/kids), comfort + characters for kids.
5. **Packshots as sculpture.** The 2000px, perfectly lit packshots can be shown huge, cropped, on tonal grounds — an editorial object gallery rather than tiny grey cards.
6. **Department navigation as a moment**, not four JPG tiles: large typographic department index with image reveal on hover (desktop) and swipeable panels (mobile).
7. **Promotions with design discipline.** Price as typography (49.90 set big, in the brand's type), a clear end date, no screaming red slab.
8. **Kids as a world of its own** — the colour-block plinth photography (yellow, purple, sky) is Gali's most distinctive visual asset; give it a dedicated, playful but controlled section.
9. **Brand house clarity.** Gali first; the Brill chain switcher demoted to the footer/utility bar.
10. **Club value made concrete.** Super Friends as numbers: 10% back, 50 ₪ welcome, 20% birthday.
11. **Store network as trust.** Regions list + "find a branch" instead of generic USP icons.
12. **Accessibility native, not overlay:** semantic headings, real text, focus states, reduced-motion.
