## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS, GSAP only for scroll-driven moments (specified by the user). Homepage only, no backend.

## Users

Primary: Israeli families shopping footwear online — often a parent buying for themselves and their kids, on mobile, price- and promo-aware. Secondary: portfolio reviewers evaluating the studio's work.

## Product Purpose

Unofficial concept redesign of gali.co.il (Israeli family footwear chain, est. 1975, part of Brill group) showing what Gali's digital flagship could feel like in 2026. Success = a convincing, shippable-feeling homepage that is materially better than the current site.

## Positioning

Israel's family shoe chain since 1975: one store for women, men and kids; own label plus international brands (Hush Puppies, Caterpillar, Lee Cooper, Easy Spirit…); licensed kids' characters; accessible prices and strong promotions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hebrew, RTL-first. Latin only for brand names.
- Real products, prices and imagery from the public site only (`research/products.json`, `public/assets/`). No invented products, reviews, store counts or claims.
- Commerce actions (cart, search, wishlist) are visual stand-ins.

## Brand Commitments

- Gali green script wordmark; name "Gali"/"גלי"; tagline "רשת הנעליים של ישראל".
- Super Friends loyalty club (Brill).
- Real promo mechanics: free delivery over 199 ₪, free store returns, זוג שני ב-59.90, end-of-season from 49.90 ₪.

## Evidence on Hand

See `research/content-map.md` and `research/asset-sources.md`. Absent: vector logo, reviews, verified store count.

## Product Principles

1. Product and people first; typography carries campaigns, never images of text.
2. The whole family, one voice, in Hebrew.
3. Promotions are clear and honest, never shouty.
4. Fast and accessible on a mid-range phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, native semantics, keyboard, prefers-reduced-motion.
